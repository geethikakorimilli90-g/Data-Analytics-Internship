# Task 24: Excel PivotTable Basics

## 1. Project Overview
This task focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore sales dataset using Microsoft Excel PivotTables. The objective is to summarize sales data and understand sales performance across different categories, regions, and months.

## 2. Objective
- Create PivotTables to summarize sales data.
- Analyze sales by category, region, and month.
- Group order dates by month.
- Calculate total sales using the Sum of Sales function.
- Identify important patterns in monthly sales.

## 3. Tools and Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Sample Superstore Dataset
- Excel PivotTables
- Excel Charts

## 4. Dataset Description
The Sample Superstore dataset contains sales transaction details used to analyze business performance. The dataset includes order dates, product categories, regions, and sales values.

## 5. Tasks Performed

### Task 1: Sales by Category
Created a PivotTable to summarize total sales for each product category.

### Task 2: Sales by Region
Created a PivotTable to compare total sales across different regions.

### Task 3: Sales by Month
Grouped the Order Date field by month and calculated monthly sales using Sum of Sales.

### Task 4: Monthly Sales Visualization
Created a chart to visualize monthly sales and make comparisons easier.

## 6. Key Insights
- Total sales recorded in the monthly PivotTable are approximately **2,297,200.86**.
- January recorded the highest monthly sales, at approximately **227,894.86**.
- February recorded the lowest monthly sales, at approximately **154,637.80**.
- Monthly sales varied throughout the year.

This task demonstrates how Excel PivotTables can organize and summarize large datasets. The monthly sales analysis helps identify high- and low-performing months, while category-wise and region-wise summaries support better understanding of sales performance.
