# Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Task Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the Sample Superstore dataset and creating an interactive Power BI report to visualize sales and profitability performance.

The main objective is to identify important business insights from the data and present them through clear and meaningful visualizations.

## Dataset

- Dataset: Sample Superstore
- Tool Used: Power BI
- Data Type: Sales and retail transaction data

## Business Question

What factors are driving sales and profit, and where should the business focus to improve profitability?

## Key Insights

- Sales and profit performance varies across product categories and regions.
- Some sub-categories contribute more to profitability than others.
- Discounts can have an impact on overall profitability.
- Regional and state-level analysis helps identify stronger and weaker performing areas.

## Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing categories and regions.
- Review products and sub-categories with low profitability.
- Monitor discount levels to protect profit margins.
- Use regular sales and profit analysis to support business decisions.
